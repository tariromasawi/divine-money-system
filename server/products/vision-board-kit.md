### Step 2: Gather Your Images

**Sources for Images:**
- Pinterest
- Unsplash (free stock photos)
- Magazine clippings (if printing)
- Personal photos
- Screenshots of goals (bank balance, travel destinations)

**Tips:**
- Choose images that evoke EMOTION
- Include yourself in the vision (use photos of you)
- Be specific (exact car model, specific destination)
- Include words and phrases that inspire you

### Step 3: Arrange Your Board

**Layout Options:**

**Option A: Life Wheel**
Place yourself in the center, surrounded by life categories in a wheel pattern.

**Option B: Timeline**
Arrange left to right: Short-term → Medium-term → Long-term goals

**Option C: Priority Focus**
Place your #1 goal largest in the center, supporting goals around it.

**Option D: Free Flow**
Intuitive arrangement that feels right to you.

## PART 4: VISION BOARD ACTIVATION RITUAL

Your vision board is more powerful when activated with intention.

### Activation Ceremony

**What you need:**
- Your completed vision board
- A candle
- Quiet space
- 15-20 minutes

**The Ritual:**

1. **Create sacred space**
   Light your candle. Take three deep breaths.

2. **Ground yourself**
   Feel your feet on the floor. Connect to the earth.

3. **Set your intention**
   Say aloud: "I activate this vision board with the power of my intention. These dreams are already on their way to me."

4. **Touch each image**
   Place your hand on each image. Say: "This or something better is mine."

5. **Feel the emotions**
   Close your eyes. Feel how it will feel when these dreams manifest. Joy, gratitude, excitement, peace.

6. **Express gratitude**
   Say: "Thank you, Universe, for these blessings that are already mine."

7. **Close the ceremony**
   Blow out the candle. Know that it is done.

### Daily Vision Board Practice

Spend 2-5 minutes each morning:
1. Look at your vision board
2. Feel the emotions of already having these things
3. Say: "Thank you for my abundant life"
4. Go about your day in alignment

**Best locations:**
- Bedroom wall (see it first and last thing)
