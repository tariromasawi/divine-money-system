# 365 DAILY AFFIRMATION CARDS
## One Powerful Affirmation for Every Day of the Year

---

### HOW TO USE THIS DECK

Each card contains a powerful affirmation designed to shift your mindset and attract positivity into your life.

**Morning Practice:**
1. Draw one card each morning
2. Read the affirmation aloud 3 times
3. Close your eyes and feel the truth of the words
4. Carry the energy with you throughout your day

**Pro Tips:**
- Set a card as your phone wallpaper
- Write today's affirmation in your journal
- Share your favorite cards on social media
- Return to cards that resonate deeply

**When you feel stuck:**

**When you feel broke:**
Money is energy. I am shifting my energy to attract abundance now.

**When you feel heartbroken:**
My heart heals stronger than before. This pain is temporary.

**When you feel lost:**
I trust my path even when I can't see it. I am exactly where I need to be.

**When you feel like giving up:**
I have survived 100% of my hardest days. I will survive this too.

Hope is not lost, just hidden. I look for one small thing to be grateful for.
