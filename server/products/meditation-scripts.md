# GUIDED MEDITATION SCRIPTS COLLECTION
## 10 Transformational Meditation Scripts for Inner Peace

---

## HOW TO USE THESE SCRIPTS

These meditation scripts are designed for:
- Personal practice (read slowly to yourself)
- Recording your own guided meditations
- Leading group meditations
- Creating audio products

**Tips for recording:**
- Speak slowly and calmly
- Pause at "..." marks
- Use a warm, soothing tone
- Add gentle background music

## MEDITATION 1: DEEP RELAXATION (15 minutes)

Welcome to this deep relaxation meditation. Find a comfortable position, either sitting or lying down. Close your eyes... and take a deep breath in... and slowly exhale.

Allow your body to settle into this moment. There's nowhere you need to be... nothing you need to do... just be here, now.

Take another deep breath... filling your lungs completely... and as you exhale, let go of any tension you're carrying.

We're going to relax your body, part by part.

Bring your attention to your feet. Notice any tension there... and as you exhale, let it go. Your feet are now completely relaxed... heavy and warm.

Move your attention to your lower legs... your calves... notice any tightness... and release it with your breath. Your lower legs are relaxed... melting into the surface beneath you.

Feel this relaxation moving up to your thighs... your upper legs... let them go completely. They are heavy... relaxed... at ease.

Bring your awareness to your hips and pelvis... release any holding here... let everything soften.

Feel your lower back relaxing... any tension simply dissolving... your middle back releasing... your upper back letting go. Your entire back is supported... relaxed... at peace.

Notice your stomach and abdomen... let this area soften... release any holding... breathe into your belly.

Feel your chest relaxing... your heart area opening... peaceful and calm.

Bring attention to your shoulders... often we carry so much tension here... let your shoulders drop... release any burden you've been carrying... let it go.

Feel relaxation flowing down your arms... your upper arms... your elbows... your forearms... your wrists... your hands... all the way to your fingertips. Your arms are heavy... relaxed... at rest.

Relax your neck... let go of any stiffness... your throat softens.

Feel your face relaxing... your jaw unclenches... your mouth softens... your cheeks relax... your eyes rest easily in their sockets... your forehead smooths... your scalp releases.

Your entire body is now deeply relaxed... from the top of your head to the tips of your toes... you are at peace.

In this state of deep relaxation... you are safe... you are supported... you are exactly where you need to be.

Stay here for a few moments... enjoying this peace... this stillness... this calm.

[Pause for 2-3 minutes]

Now, gently begin to bring your awareness back to the room... wiggle your fingers and toes... take a deep breath... and when you're ready, slowly open your eyes.

You are relaxed... refreshed... at peace.

## MEDITATION 2: MORNING INTENTION SETTING (10 minutes)

Good morning. Welcome to this new day filled with possibility.

Find a comfortable seated position... close your eyes... and take three deep breaths.

Inhale possibility... exhale anything that doesn't serve you.
Inhale gratitude... exhale tension.
Inhale peace... exhale worry.

This is a brand new day. A fresh start. A blank page.

Take a moment to appreciate the gift of this morning... the gift of being alive... the gift of another day to create, to love, to grow.

Now, set your intention for today.

Ask yourself: How do I want to feel today?

[Pause]

Hold that feeling in your heart... let it expand... feel it in every cell of your body.

Ask yourself: What is most important for me to focus on today?

[Pause]

Trust the answer that comes. Your inner wisdom knows.

Visualize your day unfolding beautifully... see yourself moving through the day with ease... handling challenges gracefully... connecting with others meaningfully... accomplishing what matters most.

See yourself at the end of this day, feeling satisfied... fulfilled... grateful.

Now, affirm your intention:

"Today, I choose to feel _______________."
"Today, I focus on _______________."
"Today, I am _______________."

Carry this intention with you throughout the day. It is your compass... your guide... your anchor.

Take one more deep breath... filling yourself with positive energy for the day ahead.

When you're ready, open your eyes... and step into your day with purpose and presence.

## MEDITATION 3: STRESS RELEASE (12 minutes)

Welcome. If you're feeling stressed or overwhelmed, you're in the right place.

Find a comfortable position... close your eyes... and take a deep breath in through your nose... hold for a moment... and exhale slowly through your mouth.

Again... breathe in calm... hold... breathe out stress.

One more time... breathe in peace... hold... breathe out tension.

I want you to imagine all your stress as a color... any color that feels right... see this colored stress in your body... where is it located? Your shoulders? Your chest? Your stomach?

Now, imagine a healing white light entering through the top of your head... this light is powerful... it dissolves stress... it brings peace.

Watch as this white light moves through your body... wherever it touches the colored stress, the stress dissolves... disappearing completely.

The light moves down through your forehead... dissolving tension.
Through your face... releasing any tightness.
Down your neck... melting stress.
Across your shoulders... the stress simply vanishes.
Down your arms... to your fingertips... peace flows.
Through your chest... your heart feels lighter.
Through your stomach... calm settles in.
Down your back... relaxation spreads.
Through your hips... legs... feet... toes.

Your entire body is now filled with white healing light. There is no stress... only peace... only calm... only clarity.

Take a moment to enjoy this feeling of complete relief.

[Pause for 1-2 minutes]

Now, I want you to remember this feeling. This peace is always available to you. Whenever stress begins to build, you can close your eyes, take three deep breaths, and return to this calm.

Stress is temporary. Peace is your natural state.

Slowly bring your awareness back to the room... take a deep breath... and open your eyes.

You are calm. You are centered. You are at peace.

## MEDITATION 4: ABUNDANCE & PROSPERITY (15 minutes)

Welcome to this abundance meditation. Today, we open our hearts and minds to the unlimited prosperity of the universe.

Find a comfortable position... close your eyes... and take three deep, centering breaths.

Begin to relax your body... let go of any tension... create space for abundance to flow.

The universe is infinitely abundant. There is more than enough for everyone, including you. You are meant to thrive.

Imagine yourself standing in a beautiful garden of abundance. Everything here represents prosperity... the trees are laden with golden fruit... the flowers bloom in every color... the air is warm and welcoming.

You walk through this garden, knowing that everything here is available to you. There is no scarcity... no competition... only abundance.

As you walk, you come to a beautiful fountain. But instead of water, this fountain flows with golden light... the light of prosperity... of wealth... of success.

You step into the fountain. The golden light surrounds you... it washes over you... it fills you completely.

As this light fills you, repeat these words in your mind:

"I am worthy of abundance."
"Money flows to me easily."
"I am a magnet for prosperity."
"Wealth is my natural state."
"I attract success effortlessly."

Feel these truths in every cell of your body. You are abundant. You have always been abundant. Any thoughts of lack are simply old programming that no longer serves you.

Now, visualize your life filled with abundance...

See your bank account growing... numbers increasing... financial freedom.
See yourself doing work you love... being paid generously for your gifts.
See your home... beautiful, comfortable, filled with everything you need.
See your relationships... rich with love and support.
See yourself giving generously... knowing there is always more than enough.

This is your abundant life. It is real. It is yours. It is now.

Take a deep breath and absorb this vision into your being.

Before you leave the garden, pick one piece of golden fruit. This represents the first step toward your abundant life. Hold it in your hands. Know that taking aligned action activates the abundance waiting for you.

Now, slowly bring your awareness back to this room... carry the feeling of abundance with you... and when you're ready, open your eyes.

You are abundant. You are prosperous. You are blessed.

## MEDITATION 5: SELF-LOVE & HEALING (15 minutes)

Welcome. This meditation is a gift you give yourself... a practice of deep self-love and healing.

Find a comfortable position... close your eyes... and place both hands over your heart.

Feel the warmth of your hands... feel your heartbeat... this heart has been beating for you your entire life, never asking for anything in return.

Take a deep breath... and as you exhale, soften into this moment.

Today, we heal the relationship with the most important person in your life... yourself.

Think of a time when you were hard on yourself... when you criticized yourself... when you didn't treat yourself with kindness. We all have these moments.

Now, imagine your younger self standing before you... the child version of you... innocent, vulnerable, deserving of love.

Look into their eyes. This child is still within you. They need your love.

Say to this younger version of yourself:

"I see you. I love you. You are enough."

Watch as your younger self begins to smile... to relax... to feel safe in your love.

Now, say:

"I'm sorry for the times I was hard on you. I didn't know better. I forgive myself, and I forgive you."

Feel the healing that comes from these words.

Say:

"From this moment forward, I choose to love you unconditionally. I will speak kindly to you. I will take care of you. I will celebrate you."

See your younger self running toward you, embracing you. Hold them close. You are one. You are whole. You are loved.

Now, bring your awareness back to your adult self, hands on heart.

Repeat these affirmations of self-love:

"I love myself completely."
"I am worthy of love and belonging."
"I forgive myself for past mistakes."
"I treat myself with kindness and compassion."
"I am enough, exactly as I am."

Feel these truths radiating from your heart... filling your entire body... overflowing into the world around you.

You cannot truly love others until you love yourself. This self-love is not selfish... it is essential.

Take a deep breath of self-compassion... and slowly open your eyes.

You are loved. You are worthy. You are whole.

## BONUS: Quick 2-Minute Calm Down Meditation

When you need immediate calm:

Close your eyes.
Breathe in for 4 counts.
Hold for 4 counts.
Exhale for 4 counts.
Hold for 4 counts.
Repeat 4 times.

Say: "I am calm. I am safe. All is well."

Open your eyes. You are centered.
