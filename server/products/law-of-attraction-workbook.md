## PART 1: UNDERSTANDING THE LAW

### The Core Principle

**Like attracts like.**

Your dominant thoughts and feelings create your reality. If you focus on lack, you attract more lack. If you focus on abundance, you attract more abundance.

This isn't magic—it's physics. Your thoughts are energy vibrations that attract matching vibrations.

### The Manifestation Formula

**DESIRE + BELIEF + ALIGNED ACTION = MANIFESTATION**

1. **Desire**: Get clear on what you want
2. **Belief**: Know it's possible and meant for you
3. **Aligned Action**: Take inspired steps

All three must be present.

### The Emotional Guidance Scale

Your emotions tell you if you're attracting what you want or what you don't want.

**High Vibration (Attracting Good):**
- Joy, appreciation, freedom
- Love, passion, enthusiasm
- Optimism, hope, contentment

**Low Vibration (Attracting Unwanted):**
- Worry, doubt, frustration
- Anger, blame, revenge
- Fear, grief, despair

**Your goal:** Spend more time in high vibration emotions.

## BONUS: Manifestation Troubleshooting

**"Nothing is happening."**
→ Are you truly believing, or secretly doubting? Clean up resistance.

**"I keep attracting the opposite."**
→ You're focused on the lack of it. Focus on having it instead.

**"I'm getting impatient."**
→ Impatience = doubt. Trust the timing and find joy now.

**"I manifested it, then lost it."**
→ Your beliefs reverted. Maintain your new identity.
