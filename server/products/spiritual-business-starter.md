## MODULE 5: TEMPLATES & SCRIPTS

### Discovery Call Script

**Opening (1-2 minutes):**
"Thank you for being here, [Name]. I'm excited to learn about you. Before we dive in, what made you reach out?"

**Exploration (10-15 minutes):**
"Tell me about where you are right now in [area you help with]..."
"What have you tried before?"
"What's holding you back from [desired outcome]?"
"If we were talking 6 months from now, what would need to happen for you to feel amazing?"

**Bridge (2-3 minutes):**
"Based on what you've shared, I believe I can help you achieve [their goal]. Here's how I work..."

**Offer (2-3 minutes):**
"I have a [package name] that's perfect for where you are. It includes... The investment is..."

**Handling Objections:**
"I need to think about it" → "Of course. What specifically do you need to think about?"
"I can't afford it" → "I understand. If money weren't an issue, would you move forward?"
"I need to ask my partner" → "Absolutely. What questions might they have that I can help answer now?"

**Close:**
"So, are you ready to [transformation]?"
