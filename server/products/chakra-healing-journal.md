## INTRODUCTION TO YOUR CHAKRAS

The seven main chakras are energy centers that run along your spine. When balanced, energy flows freely, supporting physical health, emotional wellbeing, and spiritual growth.

When blocked, you may experience physical symptoms, emotional imbalances, and life stagnation.

This journal guides you through one week per chakra, with exercises to identify blockages and restore balance.

# WEEK 1: ROOT CHAKRA (Muladhara)

## Location: Base of Spine
## Color: Red
## Element: Earth
## Mantra: LAM

### Understanding Your Root Chakra

The root chakra governs your sense of safety, security, and belonging. It's your foundation.

**When balanced:** You feel grounded, safe, secure, and stable. You trust life.

**When blocked:** You experience fear, anxiety, insecurity, financial stress, or feeling ungrounded.

## Location: Below Navel

### Understanding Your Sacral Chakra

The sacral chakra governs emotions, creativity, pleasure, and sexuality. It's your creative life force.

**When balanced:** You flow with life, feel creative, experience pleasure without guilt, and have healthy emotional expression.

**When blocked:** You experience emotional numbness, creative blocks, guilt around pleasure, or unhealthy relationships.
