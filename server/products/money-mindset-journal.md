## INTRODUCTION

Your relationship with money was formed before you could even count it.

By age 7, you absorbed beliefs about money from your parents, culture, and experiences. These beliefs run like invisible software, determining how much you earn, save, spend, and receive.

This journal guides you through 21 days of deep money mindset transformation. You'll:
- Uncover hidden beliefs blocking your wealth
- Release money trauma and shame
- Install new beliefs that attract abundance
- Create a prosperity consciousness that lasts

Let's reprogram your money story.
